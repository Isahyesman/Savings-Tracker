Privacy Policy

Savings Tracker

Last updated: July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Savings Tracker ("the App") collects, how it is used, and how it is protected. The App is a private savings, loan, and record-keeping tool used by a specific business and its registered clients — it is not a public financial service, and accounts are created only by an authorized administrator.

Information We Collect

The App collects and stores the following information, entered either by an administrator when setting up a client account, or generated through normal use of the App:

  • Client full name, ID number, and phone number
  • Login username and password (for both administrators and clients)
  • Savings deposit and withdrawal records
  • Loan and loan repayment records
  • Messages sent between a client and an administrator within the App

We do not collect location data, contacts, photos, or any information beyond what is described above.

How Information Is Used

Information collected is used solely to:

  • Create and manage client accounts
  • Record and display savings and loan activity
  • Allow communication between clients and administrators
  • Allow an administrator to verify or recover a client's login details if requested

Data Storage and Security

All information is stored in a secured cloud database accessible only to authorized administrators of the business operating this App. We take reasonable steps to protect this information, but as with any online service, no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be 100% secure.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share client information with any third party. Information is used only within the App, for the purposes described above.

Client Rights

Clients may contact their administrator at any time to review, correct, or request deletion of their account information. Deleting an account removes login access; associated transaction records may be retained for the business's own record-keeping purposes.

Children's Privacy

This App is not directed at, or knowingly used by, children. Accounts are created only for verified clients of the operating business.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time as the App changes. Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be directed to the administrator managing your account.
